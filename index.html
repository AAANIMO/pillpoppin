BACK TO LANDING ▸
OVERDOSE ⚠ ATTENZIONE

OVERDOSE builds a random stack of 5–7 effects with extreme parameters and fires it straight onto the current selection (or the whole buffer, if nothing is selected).

Risks:

  • Wipes the effects chain you've built so far.
  • Can corrupt the TIFF header → SIGNAL LOST and no decodable output.
  • The result is unpredictable: you might get something beautiful, or a black block. That's the nature of databending.

Tip: nothing is committed until you hit APPLY, and UNDO (Cmd+Z) takes you back to the previous layer.

RESET IMAGE? ⚠ DESTRUCTIVE

RESET wipes everything — the current image, the effects chain, your selection and the entire layer history. None of it can be recovered.

Export first if you want to keep anything: DL .TIFF / .PNG / .JPG. This session is never saved by the browser.
